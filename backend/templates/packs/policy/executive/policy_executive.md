## Acceptable Use Policy — At a Glance

- **Applies to: **All employees, contractors, and authorized third parties with access to {{ORGANIZATION_NAME}} systems.
- **What it does: **Defines permitted and prohibited use of company technology resources.

## Purpose

Establish a single, executive-approved standard for how organization technology is used. Reduce the risk of accidental data loss, insider misuse, and regulatory exposure.

## Scope

All Authorized Users (employees, contractors, third parties). All Organization information resources (devices, networks, cloud services, code, data).

## Policy Statements (Top 6)

| # | Requirement | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Credentials are personal. No sharing of passwords, MFA, or session tokens. | Credential sharing is the #1 cause of preventable breaches. |
| 2 | Approved tools only. Use the catalog; request additions through the standard process. | Unapproved tools bypass our vendor-risk and data-handling controls. |
| 3 | Sensitive data stays in sanctioned systems. No personal cloud, no consumer messaging. | A single exfiltration path defeats every other control. |
| 4 | No bypassing security tools. Endpoint agents, MFA, content filters stay on. | Tools off = audit findings on. |
| 5 | Report incidents fast. Lost device, suspected phish, suspicious access — within 4 hours. | Speed compresses regulatory notification windows. |
| 6 | Use of generative AI for sensitive data is governed by the AI Acceptable Use Standard. | AI tools are the newest data-handling channel; treat them like any other. |

## Enforcement

Honest mistakes: coaching. Repeated or intentional violations: progressive discipline up to termination. Criminal conduct: referred to law enforcement.

## Exceptions

Submit to {{POLICY_OWNER}} with business justification + compensating control. Decision in five business days. Maximum duration twelve months. Tracked in the Exception Register.
