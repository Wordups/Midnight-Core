## HIPAA Privacy Training — At a Glance

- **Audience: **Every workforce member with PHI access. Engineering, customer success, ops, security, contractors.
- **Duration: **Approximately 60 minutes plus a knowledge check.
- **Why it matters: **HIPAA Security Rule 164.308(a)(5) requires it. OCR audits look at completion rates.
- **Owner: **{{POLICY_OWNER}} | Recertify annually | Next review: {{NEXT_REVIEW_DATE}}

## What the Learner Walks Away With

1. Knows what PHI is (the 18 identifiers) and what isn't (de-identified data).
2. Understands we're a Business Associate and what that means for our obligations.
3. Applies minimum-necessary in daily work.
4. Knows the difference between an incident and a Breach.
5. Knows how and where to report quickly — and that we don't retaliate.

## Knowledge Check

Five questions, mixed format. Passing score: 80%. Three failed attempts within 90 days triggers an instructor-led review with the Privacy Officer.

## Completion Criteria

- Complete all six content sections in order.
- Score 80%+ on the knowledge check.
- Acknowledge the HIPAA Privacy Policy and this module in the LMS.
- Recorded in workforce training record; reported to Approver quarterly.

## Recertification

Annual recertification with year-over-year content updates. 30-day grace before manager escalation. 60 days triggers access review.
