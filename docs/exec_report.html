Paid general availability

Tiered pricing anchored on questionnaire volume and evidence corpus size. The autonomous build loop keeps shipping features while you sell.

  • Starter / Team / Enterprise with plan-fenced limits (already enforced in code)
  • MCP server as an enterprise hook — "operate your compliance workspace from your own agents"
  • Land-and-expand: vendor assessments → internal audits → continuous monitoring

Positioning: answers from your own evidence, with citations — or it says it doesn't know.

Every competitor auto-fills questionnaires with plausible text. Midnight retrieves from the tenant's real corpus, cites the exact quote, and fails closed to "insufficient evidence" rather than bluffing an auditor. That refusal is the product — it's what makes the output defensible in a real audit.
